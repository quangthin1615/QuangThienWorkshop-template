## Self-Assessment

From **July 25, 2026 to September 28, 2026**, I participated in the **Workforce Bootcamp – First Cloud AI Journey** program organized by **Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam**. During the program, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge acquired at **Hanoi University of Civil Engineering** to practical activities related to **cloud computing and AWS infrastructure**.

During the program, I completed a series of learning modules and hands-on labs related to AWS cloud infrastructure, including services such as **Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, AWS IAM, Amazon VPC, Amazon RDS, AWS Lambda, Amazon CloudWatch, Auto Scaling, AWS CloudFormation, Docker/ECS, and CI/CD**. Through these learning and hands-on activities, I strengthened my knowledge of **cloud computing, system configuration, resource management, and security on AWS**, while also improving my self-learning, technical research, teamwork, and technical report writing skills.

In particular, during **Workshop 5**, I completed practical activities including **deploying a VPC and Subnets, configuring Security Groups, setting up an IAM Role, deploying an EC2 instance, installing and configuring the CloudWatch Agent, collecting CloudWatch Logs, configuring CloudWatch Alarms, setting up Amazon SNS notifications, testing the system, and cleaning up resources after completing the lab**.

Through these hands-on exercises, I gained a better understanding of how to build and manage a basic system on AWS, as well as how **Networking, Compute, Identity and Access Management, Monitoring, and Notification** components work together. This process also helped me improve my **troubleshooting** skills, identify configuration problems, and proactively search for technical documentation to resolve issues encountered during the practical exercises.
