## Prepare the AWS Account

1. Sign in to the AWS Management Console.
2. Verify access to EC2, VPC, IAM, CloudWatch, and SNS.
3. Check that the required permissions are available.
4. Confirm the selected Region is `ap-southeast-2`.

## Result

The AWS account is ready for the monitoring lab.
