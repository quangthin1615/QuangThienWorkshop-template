title: "5.11.2 Check Alarm Activation"

weight: 2

---

1. Open **CloudWatch → Alarms**.

2. Select `High-CPU-Alarm-Monitoring-Lab`.

3. Wait approximately 5–7 minutes and refresh.

4. Monitor the Alarm changing from `OK` to `In alarm`.

5. Check that CPUUtilization exceeds the 70% threshold.

In practice, CPU reached approximately 87.2% and was higher at some points.
