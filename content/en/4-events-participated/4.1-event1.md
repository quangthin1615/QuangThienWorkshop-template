## 1. Event Name

**AWS VIETNAM COMMUNITY MEETUP**

The event provides participants with an opportunity to connect with the community and update their knowledge of AWS, AI, and AI Agents.

## 2. Time and Location

- **Time:** Saturday, July 25, 2026, 08:30 - 12:00.
- **Location:** Grand Terra.
- **Address:** 36 Cat Linh, O Cho Dua, Hanoi, Vietnam.

## 3. My Role

**Role:** Participant.

I participated in check-in, networking, followed the sharing sessions, interacted with the community, took a group photo, and participated in the Kahoot Quiz & Lucky Draw.

## 4. Main Content of the Event

### 4.1. Check-in & Networking

**Time:** 08:30 - 09:00

Participants checked in, got to know each other, and interacted with community members before the program started.

**Lesson Learned:** Networking helps expand relationships and creates opportunities to exchange knowledge with people who share an interest in technology.

### 4.2. Community Introduction

**Time:** 09:00 - 09:20

**Speakers:** Anh Ho & Phong Pham

This session introduced community activities and provided an environment for members to interact, share knowledge, and connect with each other.

**Lesson Learned:** The technology community is a useful environment for learning from experience and keeping up with new trends.

### 4.3. OpenClaw – The Rise and Practice of Open-Source AI Agents

**Time:** 09:20 - 09:50

**Speaker:** Tuan Vu

The session introduced OpenClaw and the development of open-source AI Agents, from architecture and how Agents use tools/skills to practical applications.

The presentation also discussed risks such as **Skill Injection, Prompt Injection and Privilege Escalation**, while emphasizing principles such as **Least Privilege, Execution Isolation and Human-in-the-loop**.

**Lesson Learned:** AI Agents can perform many tasks but need to have access permissions and security controlled from the design stage.

### 4.4. From AI Trends to Business Value

**Time:** 09:50 - 10:20

**Speakers:** Nguyen Thu & Nam La

The presentation focused on transforming AI trends into practical value for businesses, especially in business operations and Sales.

**Lesson Learned:** When applying AI, it is necessary to consider not only the technology but also the practical value that AI can create for businesses.

### 4.5. Tea Break, Group Photo & Networking

**Time:** 10:20 - 10:40

Participants took a break, took a group photo, and continued interacting and exchanging ideas with community members.

**Lesson Learned:** Networking activities help strengthen connections and create opportunities to further discuss the knowledge shared during the event.

### 4.6. Ship Fast with AI, Not by

**Time:** 10:40 - 11:10

**Speaker:** Henry (Duc) Bui

The session focused on using AI to accelerate the **write → run → debug** process, while developers still need to control architecture, design, interfaces, and security.

The presentation emphasized standardizing knowledge into **lint rules, CI checks, skills and documentation** for reuse.

**Lesson Learned:** AI helps increase productivity, but developers still play an important role in design, testing, and ensuring software quality.

### 4.7. Selecting the Right AI Agent Pattern on AWS

**Time:** 11:10 - 11:40

**Speaker:** Dzung Luong

The presentation introduced how to select suitable patterns when building AI Agent systems on AWS and the factors that need to be considered during deployment.

**Lesson Learned:** When building AI Agents on AWS, it is necessary to choose an appropriate architecture and pay attention to permission, security, isolation, and system observability.

### 4.8. Kahoot Quiz & Lucky Draw

**Time:** 11:40 - 12:00

The program ended with a Kahoot Quiz and Lucky Draw, allowing participants to interact and review the content that had been shared.

**Lesson Learned:** Interactive activities help reinforce knowledge and create a connected atmosphere among members.

## 5. Lessons Learned

After participating in the event, I gained several lessons:

- Gained a better understanding of **AI Agents** and how Agents use tools/skills to perform tasks.
- Recognized that **security and access permissions** are important factors when building Agents.
- Understood the role of the **Least Privilege** principle in access control.
- AI can help accelerate software development, but **developers still play an important role in controlling architecture, design, and quality**.
- Knowledge and experience should be standardized into documents, rules, or tools so they can be reused.

## 6. Personal Contribution

I fully participated in the program, actively followed the sharing sessions, recorded knowledge about AWS, AI, and AI Agents, and participated in networking and interactive activities throughout the event.
